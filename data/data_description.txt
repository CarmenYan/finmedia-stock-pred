NEWS: One row per news article about a stock, collected from the Alpaca News API.

    ticker: Stock symbol of the company the article was retrieved for.
           AAPL	Apple Inc. (Information Technology)
           JNJ	Johnson & Johnson (Health Care)
           ADD MORE SOON

    created_at: Date and time the article was published, in UTC (Jan 2020 – Oct 2026). e.g. 2026-10-16 13:14:35+00:00

    headline: Title of the news article. e.g. "Apple Hits $5 Trillion Market Cap as Nvidia's ..."

    content: Full text of the news article, in HTML (empty for some short news items).

    source: News provider that published the article.
           benzinga	Benzinga, a US financial news outlet

    symbols: List of all stock symbols tagged on the article. e.g. [AAPL, META, MSFT]

    sent_score: (derived) Net sentiment of the article content from FinBERT, positive confidence minus negative confidence
            ranging from -1 (most negative) to 1 (most positive)

PRICES: One row per stock per trading day, collected from the Alpaca Market Data API.

    symbol: Stock symbol.
           AAPL	Apple Inc. (Information Technology)
           JNJ	Johnson & Johnson (Health Care)
           ADD MORE SOON
           SPY	SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (used as a reference for overall market movement)

    timestamp: Trading day, stored as midnight US Eastern time in UTC (Jan 2020 – Oct 2026). e.g. 2020-01-02 05:00:00+00:00

    open: Adjusted opening price of the day, in USD (adjusted for splits and dividends).

    high: Adjusted highest price of the day, in USD.

    low: Adjusted lowest price of the day, in USD.

    close: Adjusted closing price of the day, in USD.

    volume: Number of shares traded during the day.

    trade_count: Number of individual trades during the day.

    vwap: Volume-weighted average price of the day, in USD.

    target: Whether the stock's closing price rises on the next trading day.
           1	next trading day's close > today's close
           0	next trading day's close <= today's close
